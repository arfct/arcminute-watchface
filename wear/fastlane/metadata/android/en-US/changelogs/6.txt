Complications now sit in a small circle on the hand: just past its tip, or at the far side of the screen where the hand starts. Choose which under Complication. They are no longer cut off on smaller watches.

Numerals and 24-hour time are now their own settings pages, and reset to Large and 12-hour once.

In 24-hour mode the dial now reads 0 to 23. Midnight shows 0 rather than 24. Thanks to Eric Migicovsky for the suggestion.
