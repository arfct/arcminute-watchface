Chronology privacy policy

Effective 2026-08-21.

Chronology is a watch face for Wear OS, published by Artifact.

Chronology does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal data or any other data. It has no network access, no analytics, no advertising, no accounts, and it requests no permissions.

The face reads the current time and your watch’s 12/24-hour setting from the system in order to draw itself. That information never leaves the watch.
