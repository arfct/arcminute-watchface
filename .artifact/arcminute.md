Arcminute keeps one hand, the hour hand, and enlarges the dial beneath it until most of the dial is off the watch. What stays on screen is the section around the current hour, with its numeral and the minute marks beside it, and at that scale the hand's place between two numerals is enough to read the minutes.

The dial comes in two styles, large numerals or a classic scale with smaller numerals and finer marks, and the colors of the background, the dial face and the hand can each be set. In 24-hour mode the numbering runs from 0 to 23. On Wear OS there is a slot for one complication, placed at the tip of the hand or at its base.

The face began on Pebble, where it went by the name Chronology until September 2026. That version is on the Rebble and RePebble stores at 2.6.0, built for seven Pebble models; the Wear OS version is on Google Play.

C and Clay for Pebble; Watch Face Format XML, generated by a Python script, for Wear OS. 2025.
