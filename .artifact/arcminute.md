Arcminute is a watch face for Pebble and Wear OS. It draws a clock dial wider than the screen and shows only the section around the current hour, with the numeral, the minute marks beside it and one hand. The hand is an hour hand, and its position between two numerals gives the minutes.

There are two dial styles, large numerals and a classic scale with smaller numerals and finer marks. The colors of the background, the dial face and the hand can be set. In 24-hour mode the dial is numbered 0 to 23. The Wear OS version has a slot for one complication, placed at the tip or the base of the hand.

The Pebble version came first and was called Chronology until September 2026. It is on the Rebble and RePebble stores at version 2.6.0 and is built for seven Pebble models. The Wear OS version is on Google Play.

C and Clay for Pebble; Watch Face Format XML, generated by a Python script, for Wear OS. 2025.
